## 2. What we collect and why
| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Name, mobile number, email, home address, password (stored only as a one-way hash) | Create and secure your account, deliver orders |
| Medicines you add, dosage times, stock left | Send dose reminders and low-stock alerts (**health data**) |

## 3. Who sees your data
- **Pharmacies** you order from or send a prescription to: your name, phone, delivery address, order items, and prescription.
- **Service providers** that work for us: SMS provider ([SMS PROVIDER]), email provider ([EMAIL PROVIDER]), Google Maps Platform (location look-ups), payment gateway ([PAYMENT PROVIDER]), hosting ([HOSTING PROVIDER]).
- Authorities, where the law requires it.
We do not sell your data.

## 4. How long we keep it
Account data until you delete your account. Prescriptions until you delete them or your account. Order and payment
records for [RETENTION PERIOD] for accounting and legal reasons, with personal details removed after you delete your account.

## 5. Your choices and rights
- **Consent:** you can turn reminders (SMS/email) and location on or off in the app at any time.
- **Access:** download a copy of your data from your account.
- **Deletion:** delete your account in the app; we remove your profile, medicines and prescriptions.
- **Correction and complaints:** contact the Grievance Officer above.

## 6. Security
Passwords are hashed, traffic is encrypted (HTTPS), prescriptions are only shown to you and the pharmacy you chose.
No system is perfectly secure; we will tell affected users and the authorities as the law requires.

## 7. Children
MediSync is for adults. If you order for a child, you are responsible for their data.
